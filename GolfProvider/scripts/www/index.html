Playing

Drag your ball back to aim -- the arrow is the shot -- and let go. Drag anywhere else, or with the right button, to look round; the wheel zooms. A shot starts along the ground, a long pull lifts it a little, and bringing the camera down while you pull lofts it more. O settings. Roll through a gold box for a power: E arms it, a click puts it down where you point (pads, springs and winds go the way you look). Let go of the right button while the left holds a shot or a power to put it down. Everyone plays the hole at once: lowest total over the course wins. In the lobby the host's Enter starts; in a round the host's Esc abandons it.
